**1\. Type of Business Activity**
---------------------------------

Your chosen business activity is one of the main factors influencing company formation expenses. Dubai offers different licenses for commercial, professional, industrial, and other business activities.

For example, a consultancy business may require a professional license, while a trading company needs a commercial license. Certain regulated activities may also require additional approvals from relevant government authorities.

Choosing the correct activity ensures your company operates legally and avoids unnecessary amendments or additional expenses later.

**2\. Mainland, Free Zone, or Offshore Company**
------------------------------------------------

The jurisdiction you select significantly affects your overall setup budget. Each option has different licensing requirements, benefits, and operational permissions.

**Mainland Company:** Suitable for businesses targeting the UAE domestic market, working with local customers, or exploring government-related opportunities.

**Free Zone Company:** Popular among international entrepreneurs, consultants, technology companies, and e-commerce businesses. Many Free Zones offer attractive packages and 100% foreign ownership, subject to applicable regulations.

**Offshore Company:** Generally suitable for international business structuring, holding arrangements, and specific investment purposes rather than conducting ordinary business operations within the UAE.

Compare the available options based on your business objectives instead of choosing only the cheapest package.

**3\. Number of Visas Required**
--------------------------------

Visa requirements are another important consideration when calculating your business setup budget.

Entrepreneurs may require investor visas for themselves and employment visas for their staff. Additional expenses may include medical examinations, Emirates ID applications, and immigration-related processing fees.

If you plan to sponsor family members, you should also consider the associated residency requirements.

Estimating your visa requirements early helps you select a suitable company package and manage your expenses effectively.

**4\. Office Space and Location**
---------------------------------

Your office requirements can have a considerable impact on your company formation budget.

Depending on your business activity and licensing authority, you may choose from several workspace options:

*   **Virtual Office:** Suitable for businesses that do not require dedicated physical premises, where permitted.
    
*   **Business Centre:** Provides shared workspace and professional facilities.
    
*   **Physical Office:** Ideal for companies requiring dedicated working space.
    
*   **Shop Front:** Suitable for retail and customer-facing businesses.
    
*   **Warehouse:** Necessary for certain trading, storage, and distribution activities.
    

The final cost depends on the location, property size, rental arrangement, and applicable licensing conditions.

**5\. Government Fees and Additional Approvals**
------------------------------------------------

Business registration involves various administrative and government charges. These may include trade name reservation, initial approvals, license issuance, company registration, and other applicable fees.

Some business activities also require external approvals or additional documentation.

It is important to request a detailed quotation that explains the included services and any potential additional charges.
